About Me - Behind The Code!

I'm currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Information Technology (FinTech) at Marwadi University, Rajkot. Every day, I'm working on building stronger programming fundamentals and becoming the kind of developer who understands not just how to code, but why a solution works.

The part of programming I enjoy the most is logic building. I like taking a problem, breaking it into smaller pieces, and figuring out the most efficient way to solve it. That's what keeps me interested in coding and motivates me to keep learning. Right now, I'm focused on improving my skills in Data Structures & Algorithms, Object-Oriented Programming, and Python.

I'm also exploring Artificial Intelligence, especially AI Prompt Engineering. I enjoy learning how to communicate with AI tools effectively and using them to speed up learning, brainstorm ideas, and solve problems. To me, AI isn't here to replace developers; it's a tool that helps us build better and learn faster.

Studying FinTech has introduced me to the connection between technology and finance. Learning about banking systems and financial technologies has helped me understand how software can solve real-world challenges beyond traditional applications.

One thing I believe is that growth comes from consistency. I don't try to learn everything at once. Instead, I focus on improving a little every day, strengthening my fundamentals, and staying curious about new technologies.

Outside of coding, I enjoy playing chess. It has taught me patience, strategic thinking, and the importance of planning a few moves ahead—skills that naturally influence the way I approach programming and problem-solving.

I'm still learning, still building, and always looking for opportunities to challenge myself. Every project teaches me something new, and every line of code brings me one step closer to becoming the developer I aspire to be.
